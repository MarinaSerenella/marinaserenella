Marina Serenella

Marina, boatyard and nautical assistance in Murano.

Marina Serenella has been operating for more than 30 years in Venice, in a strategic position in the Northern Lagoon, right in Murano. One reliable point of reference to moor, store and keep your boat in excellent condition.

  • Moorings for motorboats, catamarans and sailing boats.
  • Summer and winter storage with boatyard services.
  • Repairs, maintenance, painting and marine mechanics.
