Cookie Policy

Information regarding the use of cookies and similar web technologies on this website.

What Are Cookies

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files sent by visited websites to the user's device (computer, tablet, smartphone), where they are stored to be retransmitted on subsequent visits.

Cookie Types

2. Cookies Used on This Website

This website uses strictly technical and essential cookies required for proper operation, including:

  • Navigation & Session Cookies: ensure normal site navigation and page loading.
  • Language Preference Cookies: remember the user's selected language preference (Italian, English, German, French).

This website does not use advertising profiling cookies or invasive third-party tracking.

Infrastructure & Forms

3. Infrastructure Security & Forms

The website is delivered through high-performance cloud infrastructure. Contact forms are processed via secure encrypted transmission protocols (HTTPS). Essential technical security headers and session tokens are used solely to provide secure connections and prevent automated spam.

Browser Settings

4. Managing or Disabling Cookies

Users may choose to disable or block cookies by adjusting settings in their web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). Disabling essential technical cookies may impact page display or navigation functionality.
