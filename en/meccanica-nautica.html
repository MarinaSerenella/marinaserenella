Engine workshop

Mechanical assistance for safer navigation.

The mechanical component is essential for boat reliability. Marina Serenella’s workshop completes the yard’s offer with engine repair and maintenance services.

  • Marine engine repair.
  • Routine and extraordinary mechanical maintenance.
  • Checks before the boating season.
  • Option to combine storage, hull washing and other boatyard work.
Why schedule checks

It is better to act before going out on the water.

1

Reliability

A scheduled check reduces the risk of problems during navigation.

2

Safety

Checking mechanical components helps keep the boat efficient and safe.

3

Organisation

During storage it is easier to schedule the necessary work.
