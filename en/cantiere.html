Boat repairs

Technical work to keep your boat efficient.

Within the facility, Marina Serenella provides the services needed for maintenance and repair of wooden and fiberglass boats, supported by shipwright work and a mechanical workshop.

Shipwright work

Restoration, adaptation and maintenance on boat components and structures.

Wood

Repairs and maintenance on wooden boats and finishes, with attention to appearance and durability.

Fiberglass

Repairs, maintenance and operations on fiberglass parts and hulls.

Hull washing

Hull cleaning and washing to improve the underwater section and plan any required work.

Mechanics

Mechanical workshop for engine repairs and maintenance.

Preparation

Pre-season checks and work to bring the boat back to navigation condition.

Working method

Assessment, work, delivery.

Inspection

The condition of the boat is analysed and the necessary type of intervention is defined.

Planning

Times, materials, operations and related services are agreed.

Execution

The yard carries out the agreed work and prepares the boat for delivery.
