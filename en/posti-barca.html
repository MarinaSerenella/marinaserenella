Moorings in Murano

A convenient marina for enjoying your boat in Venice.

Marina Serenella offers up to 60 berths for motorboats, catamarans and sailing boats. Its position in the Northern Lagoon makes the marina a practical base for those sailing in Venice and looking for support services throughout the year.
