Marine finishes

Careful painting changes the look and protects the boat.

Marine painting requires preparation, precision and attention to detail. Marina Serenella can handle aesthetic restoration, finishing and maintenance work on boats and components.

  • Painting and aesthetic restoration of motorboats and vessels.
  • Surface preparation and finishing work.
  • Work on wooden and fiberglass parts.
  • Option to combine hull washing, storage and technical checks.
When to intervene

Ideal during storage.

01

Aesthetic restoration

To renew the appearance of the boat and correct signs of wear.

02

Protection

To help protect surfaces exposed to the marine environment.

03

Value enhancement

To maintain the boat’s value and present it at its best.
